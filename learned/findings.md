## references/railroad.md

- The freight platform gets the unloaded cargo at the very end of the unload, and a reading of the platform inventory taken right after the docking ended does not show it yet. A dispatcher that drops its booking for the delivery on the "job done" event and reads the stock in the same cycle sees a platform emptier by a whole train load for a moment, and sends the next train into it (seen: a second train sent to a platform with 577 of 3200 free, two cycles after the first had unloaded there). Keep the delivered amount as stock at that platform for a few seconds after the event, clamped to the platform's capacity; the snapshot age of the stations' data decides how long (a station snapshot may be up to 10 s old in that mod, so 15 s). Evidence: dispatcher log of a closed-loop scenario, `job 4 ... to <receiver>` created with `free 577` while the station read 4223 of 4800 one cycle later. (2026-10-06, game 502094, SML 3.12.0, UE 5.6.1-CSS)
- Right after a save loads, empty belts and machines downstream of a freight platform pull far more than their nominal rate: six smelters (180/min nominal) took ore at 950–1190/min for the first minutes while their belts and input buffers filled. A rate estimator with a 60 s window reads the burst as the station's rate and needs about 15 minutes to come back to the nominal value; a forecast built on it is wrong in the safe direction for an input station (stock "runs out" 5× too early) and in the unsafe direction for an output station of the same factory. Measure for longer or discount the first minutes after a load. Evidence: `<receiver>: level 1, lasts 130 s, platforms ... at 955.7/min` at t=56 s and `at 179.4/min` at t=1320 s in the same run. (2026-10-06, game 502094, SML 3.12.0, UE 5.6.1-CSS)
- `ETrainDockingState` of a train that stood at a station as `TDS_ReadyToDock` falls back to `TDS_None` the moment the train drives away from that station, not only when a docking finishes. Code that takes "`TDS_None` after I started a move" as "the move is done" ends the move a second after it began; check that the train is under its destination station as well. Seen as a move command re-issued every 10 s while the train stood at another station, with every other check green. Evidence: `Driver: move <train> to <station A>` every cycle while the status line read `at <station B>`. (2026-10-06, game 502094, SML 3.12.0, UE 5.6.1-CSS)
- Cost of the game's own route search on a small layout (8 stations, 52 signals, 5 trains): a dispatcher that asks about every pair of stations and every free place in its first cycle made 54–60 searches in the game's graph, which took 16–20 frames spread over the first cycle; later questions came from the mod's memory at about 20 µs each (36 587 answers from memory in a 34-minute run). Ask the game once per pair and remember the answer. Evidence: `routes: 54 searched in the game's graph, 36587 from memory, 20.2 us a search, longest 399.4 us`. (2026-10-06, game 502094, SML 3.12.0, UE 5.6.1-CSS)

## references/patterns-and-pitfalls.md

- A timer callback that ends a test with `FPlatformMisc::RequestExit(false, ...)` and then goes on with its work crashed the game with an access violation inside the same callback, in three runs, after the result had been logged (the crash stacks name only the lambda under `FTimerManager::Tick`; which object was gone is not known). Return right after the call that ends the run, and check the "ended" flag between the steps of the callback; clearing the timer from inside its own callback is fine but does not stop the current invocation. Evidence: `CrashContext.runtime-xml` of three runs, `EXCEPTION_ACCESS_VIOLATION reading address 0xffffffffffffffff`, stack `...FLoopTest::Start::<lambda_2>::operator()() ← FTimerManager::Tick()`; no crash in 7 runs with the checks. (2026-10-06, game 502094, SML 3.12.0, UE 5.6.1-CSS)

## references/tools.md

- Drawing a layout from a log dump in PowerShell: `$Matches` is overwritten by every `-match`, also the one inside an `if` further down the loop, so copy it (`$m = $Matches`) right after the match you need, or a nested test like `$text -match 'platform'` silently loses the fields of the outer match (14 of 50 tracks vanished from a drawing that way). Numbers written into SVG with `"$x"` follow the current culture: a Polish system writes `12,5` and the SVG is invalid; set `[CultureInfo]::CurrentCulture = [CultureInfo]::InvariantCulture` at the top of the script or format with `.ToString([CultureInfo]::InvariantCulture)`. A quick preview of the SVG without a browser tool: `msedge --headless=new --disable-gpu --hide-scrollbars --window-size=W,H --screenshot=out.png file:///path.svg`. (2026-10-06, Windows 11, PowerShell 5.1, Edge)
