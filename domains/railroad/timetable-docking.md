# Railways: timetable, docking, driving a train through its timetable

Part of the `satisfactory-modding` skill, railroad domain (index: `domains/railroad/README.md`). Markers: no marker = verified in practice, **[source]** = read from SML or game code, not tested, **[?]** = uncertain. Replace the parts in `<...>` with your own.

## Timetable and docking

- `AFGRailroadTimeTable` (`FGRailroadTimeTable.h`): `SetStops` (returns `bool`), `SetCurrentStop`, `GetStops`, `GetCurrentStop`, `GetNumStops`. `SetStops({})` clears it (returns `true`). **[source]** `AddStop`, `RemoveStop`, `IncrementCurrentStop`. The limit is 100 stops (`MAX_STOPS`).
- A stop is an `FTimeTableStop`: a station (`AFGTrainStationIdentifier`) and docking rules (`FTrainDockingRuleSet` in `FGTrainDockingRules.h`, all fields saved in the save game):
  - `DockingDefinition`: `ETrainDockingDefinition::TDD_LoadUnloadOnce` (default) or `TDD_FullyLoadUnload`,
  - `DockForDuration` (default 15 s) and `IsDurationAndRule` (default `false`),
  - `IgnoreFullLoadUnloadIfTransferBlockedByFilters`,
  - `LoadFilterDescriptors` and `UnloadFilterDescriptors`: lists of item classes.
- **A default rule set with only `DockingDefinition = TDD_FullyLoadUnload` means "full or 15 s", not "full".** The train window's Stop Settings show it as "Freight Wagon is fully loaded/unloaded" **or** "wait for (seconds): 15", with the filters under Load Only / Unload Only.
  - With that rule and an unloading platform that was nearly full, the train left with 100–200 ore still in the wagon, in three runs. The docking took 27–30 s: the platform was in `ETPDS_WaitForTransferCondition` for 2.5 s, then in `ETPDS_Unloading`.
  - The 15 s "or" timer is what lets it leave. Measured below.
- A timetable written from code is shown correctly in the train window, and the player can edit it there.

## Docking rules in practice

Measured on a freight platform with one wagon (3200 iron ore, platform 4800), rules written from code, each case run twice with the same result.

- **Timeline:** when the transfer can start at once, the platform goes straight to `ETPDS_Loading` or `ETPDS_Unloading` for exactly **27.0 s**. The whole transfer then happens in one step at the end: the platform count falls once and the wagon jumps from 0 to 3200. `TDS_None` follows in the same frame.
  - When the transfer cannot start, the platform waits in `ETPDS_WaitForTransferCondition` and checks again every **2.5 s**.
  - A rule with a timer that cannot transfer goes to `ETPDS_IdleWaitForTime`.
- **Belts keep running while the platform waits** in `ETPDS_WaitForTransferCondition` (solid cargo; for pipes on fluid platforms see below): 1200 decreases in 120 s on one layout, 2599 in 868 s on another.
  - **[?] During the 27 s load/unload phase it depends on the layout.** In a later series belts stood still in the phase in every layout built (two belts to Smelters, one belt to storage), but the test had set the platform's state at the start of the phase there; the difference to the 269 decreases below stays unexplained. A loading platform kept receiving from its input belt (+1080 ore, about 10 changes a second), and a 1-platform unloading station with one output belt into storage kept emptying (269 decreases in 27 s). A 2-platform unloading station whose two Mk.2 output belts were backed up to six Smelters did not change once in its 27 s `ETPDS_Unloading` phase: the transfer added exactly 3200 (1600 -> 4800) and the Smelters' input buffers dropped (600 -> 566). Which difference decides this is not known.
  - After that phase the belts pulled at their full speed (240/min on two Mk.2 belts, against 180/min consumption) for about 2 minutes, until the belts and buffers had refilled.
  - Code that measures a platform's outflow should treat the load/unload phase and about 2 minutes after it as invalid, but not the waiting.
  - Right after a save loads, empty belts and machines downstream of a freight platform pull far more than their nominal rate: six smelters (180/min nominal) took ore at 950–1190/min for the first minutes while their belts and input buffers filled. A rate estimator with a 60 s window reads the burst as the station's rate and needs about 15 minutes to come back to the nominal value; a forecast built on it is wrong in the safe direction for an input station (stock "runs out" 5× too early) and in the unsafe direction for an output station of the same factory. Measure for longer or discount the first minutes after a load. Evidence: `<receiver>: level 1, lasts 130 s, platforms ... at 955.7/min` at t=56 s and `at 179.4/min` at t=1320 s in the same run. (2026-10-06, game 502094, SML 3.12.0, UE 5.6.1-CSS)
  - **Pipes stop during a fluid platform's 27 s transfer phase, and run while it waits.** A fluid platform in `ETPDS_Loading` and another in `ETPDS_Unloading`, both on a pipe with a Valve at 60 m³/min, did not change once in their phase (per-frame count, 0 changes in 27 s each); before and after, the count moved by 1 m³/s. A loading fluid platform in `ETPDS_WaitForTransferCondition` kept filling from its pipe for 4 minutes (2.0 -> 2.2 million units). For belts the layout with two backed-up Mk.2 belts behaved the same way as the pipes. Evidence: two runs on a 3-platform fluid station, logs of 2026-10-05 15:40-15:46 UTC. (2026-10-05, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- The freight platform gets the unloaded cargo at the very end of the unload, and a reading of the platform inventory taken right after the docking ended does not show it yet. A mod that drops its reservation for a delivery when the docking ends and reads the stock in the same cycle sees a platform emptier by a whole train load for a moment, and sends the next train into it (seen: a second train sent to a platform with 577 of 3200 free, two cycles after the first had unloaded there). Keep the delivered amount as stock at that platform for a few seconds after the event, clamped to the platform's capacity; the snapshot age of the stations' data decides how long (in the tested mod the stations' data could be up to 10 s old, so 15 s). Evidence: dispatcher log of a closed-loop scenario, `job 4 ... to <receiver>` created with `free 577` while the station read 4223 of 4800 one cycle later. (2026-10-06, game 502094, SML 3.12.0, UE 5.6.1-CSS)
- **The 27 s load/unload phase is a per-platform timer, and it can be shortened at runtime.** `AFGBuildableTrainPlatformCargo` has protected `UPROPERTY(EditDefaultsOnly)` floats; the defaults of both `Build_TrainDockingStation_C` and `Build_TrainDockingStationLiquid_C` are `mTimeToCompleteLoad 27.0`, `mTimeToSwapLoadVisibility 20.0`, `mTimeToCompleteUnload 27.0`, `mTimeToSwapUnloadVisibility 6.0`, `mWaitForConditionUpdatePeriod 2.5` (the 2.5 s re-check of the transfer condition). Written on a placed platform by reflection (`FindFProperty<FFloatProperty>(AFGBuildableTrainPlatformCargo::StaticClass(), TEXT("mTimeToCompleteUnload"))->ContainerPtrToValuePtr<float>(Platform)`, no AccessTransformers needed), the next docking uses the new value: with `mTimeToCompleteUnload = 2` (and the swap time scaled to 0.44) the platform went `None -> Unloading`, then `Unloading -> WaitingForTransfer` after 2.0 s and `Complete` in the same frame, the wagon lost 3200 and the platform gained 3200 in that frame, and the train left docked for 2.0 s instead of 27.0 s; loading platforms left at 27 kept 27.0 s. The fields are not `SaveGame` in the header and fall back to the defaults after a load (all 12 platforms were back at 27 s in the new world after each of two loads in one process, before the mod set the timers again), so they must be set again. Evidence: a test run with 7 unloadings of 3200 iron ore at three unloading platforms, log lines `Unloading -> WaitingForTransfer after 2.0 s` and `wagon -3200, platform +3200`. (2026-10-05, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **The unloading crane's animation timeline, and how to shorten the phase without breaking it.** At a freight platform in unload mode with the default 27 s phase, screenshots once a second from the start of `ETPDS_Unloading` showed: the crane comes down and grips the wagon's container at 2–5 s, lifts it off the wagon at about 10 s, carries it sideways over the platform at 14–16 s, lowers it at 17–18 s, has it lying in the platform's storage at 19–20 s, and returns empty until 27 s. The animation does not follow `mTimeToCompleteUnload` (entry above): when the phase ends early the platform resets its look on the spot, so a 2 s phase hid the cargo from the wagon while the crane had barely moved, 13 s hid it from the crane as it started sideways, and 21 s made the crane jump back to its start after the drop. What looked right in the game: the crane at normal speed until the drop, then the platform's skeletal mesh components sped up with `USkeletalMeshComponent::GlobalAnimRateScale` (one component on `Build_TrainDockingStation_C`; set back to 1 when the phase ends), and `mTimeToCompleteUnload = drop + (27 − drop) / rate`. With drop 20 s and rate 1.5 the phase took 24.7 s, the cargo counts were exact and the crane returned smoothly (it looked too fast; rate 1.4, about 25 s, was not run). **[?]** `GlobalAnimRateScale` is a visual, client-side property: in multiplayer every client would have to set it on its own copy of the platform. Evidence: screenshot series at 1707×960, log lines `crane at 1.50x after 20.0 s: 1 animated meshes`, `Unloading -> WaitingForTransfer after 24.7 s`, `wagon -3200, platform +3200`. (2026-10-05, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **Each platform of a multi-platform station transfers on its own.** A train with 2 wagons at a station with 2 unloading platforms, "fully unload and 0 s": the platform with room unloaded its wagon 27 s after docking (`ETPDS_Unloading` -> `ETPDS_WaitingForTransfer` -> `ETPDS_Complete`, 0 -> 3200), while the other stayed in `ETPDS_WaitForTransferCondition` for 14 more minutes until it had room. The docking (`TDS_None`) ended only when the last platform finished, with both wagons empty. One blocked platform holds the whole train.
  - **A platform lets its wagon go as soon as its own transfer is done, while the train stays docked.** At a station with two unloading platforms and a train with two wagons, the platform with room unloaded, went to `ETPDS_None` and its `GetDockedActor()` became null, while the other platform kept its wagon in `ETPDS_WaitForTransferCondition` and the train stayed `TDS_Docked`. Code that pairs wagons with platforms during a docking must not expect every wagon to stay docked to its platform until the train leaves. Evidence: test log `platform 2: 3200 with wagon -1 (none docked)`, platform 1 still `ETPDS_WaitForTransferCondition`, 20 s after docking. (2026-10-05, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **What a backed-up belt line takes from a platform:** six Smelters (Iron Ingot, 30/min each) fed from one platform by two Mk.2 belts took one item at a time, 2 or 4 items in any 1 s window and exactly 6 in any 2 s window (the Smelters' 2 s cycle), 179.7/min over 700 s. With full input buffers but empty belts at the start, the same layout took 238/min in the first minute, 205/min in the second and 180/min from the third. A Smelter's input buffer holds 100 iron ore: `GetInputInventory()->AddStack(FInventoryStack(1000, Ore))` added 100.
- **"Wait until full":** `TDD_FullyLoadUnload` + `IsDurationAndRule = true` + `DockForDuration = 0`.
  - Loading with 1000 ore on the platform: the train stayed in `ETPDS_WaitForTransferCondition` for 45 s. Once the platform held a wagon's worth, it loaded 3200 in one 27 s step.
  - Unloading: it waits until the platform has **room for the whole wagon**. With 4000 on the platform it waited 120 s while the output belt drained it to 1598, then unloaded 3200 at once. It never unloads part of a wagon.
- **The game default** (`TDD_FullyLoadUnload`, `DockForDuration = 15`, `IsDurationAndRule = false`) means "full **or** 15 s":
  - with 1000 ore it loaded what was on the platform at the end of the 27 s phase (2182, because the input belt kept feeding) and left after 29.6 s,
  - at a full unloading platform it waited 2.5 s + 12.5 s idle and left after 15.0 s with the wagon untouched.
- `TDD_FullyLoadUnload` with `DockForDuration = 0` and `IsDurationAndRule = false` ("full or 0 s") behaves like load once: it loaded 1000 and left after 29.6 s.
- **Items the filter blocks:** a wagon with 1600 ore and 1600 coal stopped at a full unload that unloads only ore.
  - The ore went out in one 27 s step. Then the train stayed docked in `ETPDS_IdleWaitForTime` indefinitely, because the coal can never leave.
  - With `IgnoreFullLoadUnloadIfTransferBlockedByFilters = true` it unloaded the ore and left after 27.0 s.
- **[source]** Empty `LoadFilterDescriptors` and `UnloadFilterDescriptors` (`FGTrainDockingRules.h:36,39`) mean no filter: every item moves. A stop where a train should only stand needs `UFGNoneDescriptor` in both lists. Found in a code review; the scenarios' stand stops set both. (2026-10-04, game 502094)
- **The "nothing" filter**, `UFGNoneDescriptor::StaticClass()` (`FGNoneDescriptor.h`) as the only load filter:
  - With `TDD_LoadUnloadOnce` the train docks and the docking ends in the same frame: `TDS_Docked` and `TDS_None` together, nothing moved. Turning the autopilot off inside the `TDS_Docked` handler leaves the train parked at the station (`TS_Parked`, 0 m moved).
  - With `TDD_FullyLoadUnload` + `IsDurationAndRule = true` it never ends. The platform stays in `ETPDS_WaitForTransferCondition` with `IsFullLoad() == 0`.
  - **Exception:** a stop with "fully load/unload AND 0 s" and the "nothing" filter holds a locomotive-plus-wagon train docked for good. It ended at once for a train of two locomotives and two wagons at a station with one freight platform: `TDS_ReadyToDock`, `TDS_Docked` and `TDS_None` came in the same tick.
    - **[?]** The likely reason is that none of its wagons stands on a platform there (the second locomotive takes the only platform, see "Which vehicle stands on which platform"), so the station has nothing to wait for. Not checked further.
    - A test that needs a train stuck in a docking has to pick a train whose wagon really stands on a platform.
- **[source]** FicsIt-Networks exposes the same timetable API in Lua.
- A test can play a consumer on an unloading Freight Platform by taking items out of its inventory with `UFGInventoryComponent::Remove(ItemClass, Num)` a few times a second: a sampler that reads the platform's amount once a second measures it as a steady outflow, on top of what the belt takes. Measured: 900 a minute removed in 0.5 s steps (15 at a time) plus a 120 a minute belt gave a reading of `1020.0/min` after 30 s. Useful when the layout has no factory that consumes fast enough for a test. (2026-10-12, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **The loading crane has the same timeline as the unloading one, at freight and fluid platforms alike, so the same shortening works for both directions and both kinds.** Screenshots once a second from the start of `ETPDS_Loading` at a freight platform (27 s phase): the crane grips the platform's container at 5–8 s, carries it over the wagon at 9–16 s, sets it on the wagon at 17–19 s, holds it until about 23 s and returns at 24–27 s; the cargo's look swaps at 20 s (`mTimeToSwapLoadVisibility`). A fluid station docking one wagon at a loading and one at an unloading fluid platform showed the same timeline for both (tanks lifted at 10–11 s, set down at 19–20 s, crane back at 24–27 s); both fluid platform classes have 1 skeletal mesh component and the same 27 s timers. With every cargo platform at `mTimeToCompleteLoad = mTimeToCompleteUnload = 20 + 7/1.4 = 25` and `GlobalAnimRateScale = 1.4` from 20 s into the phase (back to 1 when the status changes), ten deliveries ran 30 phases (15 loading, 15 unloading, freight) of 25.00–25.02 s each, the rate change was seen 20.02–20.03 s into each phase, and every transfer was exact (`wagon +3200, platform -3200` / `wagon -3200, platform +3200` in one frame). A platform without room for a whole wagon still took nothing in part and unloaded in 25.00 s once it had room. The empty parts of the animation can be sped up too: the crane reaches the container on the wagon (unloading) or on the platform (loading) about 4 s into the game's phase (screenshots once a second: at 3 s still above it, at 4 s on it; the unloading swap of the cargo's look is at 6 s). With the rate 1.4 from the phase's start until 4/1.4 = 2.86 s, 1.0 until 2.86 + 16 = 18.86 s and 1.4 again after that, and the timers at 23.86 s (the swap timers moved to the same animation moments, unload 4.86 s, load 18.86 s), fluid and freight phases lasted 23.86–23.87 s with the rate changes seen at 0.02, 2.88 and 18.88 s, and a viewer in full graphics found the switches smooth. Evidence: test log lines `phase 1 crane: 1.40x at 0.02 s, 1.00x at 2.88 s, 1.40x at 18.88 s` and `fluid Loading lasted 23.87 s, its timer said 23.86 s`. Evidence: test logs of 2026-10-12, lines `phase <n>: <platform> freight Loading lasted 25.00 s, its timer said 25.00 s` and `crane 1.00x -> 1.40x, 20.02 s into Loading`. (2026-10-12, game 502094, SML 3.12.0, UE 5.6.1-CSS)

## Driving a train through its timetable

This works without hooks:

```cpp
AFGRailroadTimeTable* TimeTable = Train->GetTimeTable();
if (!TimeTable) TimeTable = Train->NewTimeTable();      // a train that never had a timetable returns nullptr
TArray<FTimeTableStop> Stops;
FTimeTableStop& Load = Stops.AddDefaulted_GetRef();
Load.Station = SupplierIdentifier;
Load.DockingRuleSet.DockingDefinition = ETrainDockingDefinition::TDD_FullyLoadUnload;  // still "or 15 s", see above
Load.DockingRuleSet.LoadFilterDescriptors.Add(ItemClass);
// ... the unloading stop the same way, with UnloadFilterDescriptors
TimeTable->SetStops(Stops);
TimeTable->SetCurrentStop(0);
Train->SetSelfDrivingEnabled(true);
```

- **Without stops:** with no timetable, or with 0 stops, `SetSelfDrivingEnabled(true)` broadcasts the autopilot and `TS_SelfDriving`. Within about 0.3 s `SDLE_NoTimeTable` follows. The AI stays `SDLS_Idle` and the train does not move.
  - **This holds a standing train still and locked for the player.** `SetSelfDrivingEnabled(true)` on a train with an empty timetable gives `SDLE_NoTimeTable`: the train brakes to a stop and stays, also on a station, and the game takes no input from the cab (the player pressed throttle in the cab for 40 s, 0 km/h). The game itself does not switch such an autopilot off. A mod can use this to keep a train out of the player's hands without any hook. The locomotive window of such a train shows the game's own warning "Locomotive has no Timetable." (screenshot, 2026-10-05). (2026-10-04, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **After `SetStops`, `SetCurrentStop(0)` and autopilot on:** on the next tick the error clears to `SDLE_NoError`, the AI goes to `SDLS_FollowPath` and `mAtcData.Path` is set. Turning the autopilot off and on in the same frame broadcasts off/on and `TS_Parked`/`TS_SelfDriving` each time.
- **Arrival, all in one frame:**
  1. `mOnDockingStateChanged(TDS_ReadyToDock)`,
  2. `mOnDockingStateChanged(TDS_Docked)`,
  3. the current stop already moves to the **next** stop (0 → 1), the path is cleared, the AI is `SDLS_Docking` and the platform is `ETPDS_Loading` or `ETPDS_WaitForTransferCondition`.
- **When the `TDS_Docked` delegate fires, `mDockedAtStation` is still null.** It is set later in the same frame. To know the station inside the delegate, remember `mAtcData.Path->Station` before docking.
- **End of the transfer:** `mOnDockingStateChanged(TDS_None)`. Then a brief `TDS_ReadyToDock` → `TDS_None` as the train looks for the path to the next stop, `mDockedAtStation` becomes null, and the AI goes back to `SDLS_FollowPath`.
- **Timing:** loading or unloading one wagon took 27–30 s from `TDS_Docked` to `TDS_None`.
- **Stopping after the last stop works from inside the `TDS_None` handler:** call `SetSelfDrivingEnabled(false)` and `SetStops({})`.
  - The train stays at the station: 0 m moved in 15 s, status `TS_Parked`, AI `SDLS_DockingCompleted`, platform `ETPDS_None`.
  - A new order from that state finds its path at once and the train leaves. Because it stands at a station, a brief `TDS_ReadyToDock` → `TDS_None` comes first.
- **An order whose first stop is the station the train stands at is served in place.** `TDS_ReadyToDock`, `TDS_Docked` and `TDS_None` came in the frame after the autopilot was turned on, with 0 m driven. Tested with an empty wagon on a full-unload stop, and with a full wagon, which was unloaded in place (a scenario's PASS, 2026-10-04, game 502094, SML 3.12.0).
- **A docking that cannot finish is not released by turning the autopilot off and clearing the timetable.** The train stays `TDS_Docked` and the platform stays in `ETPDS_WaitForTransferCondition`. The game itself switches the status to `TS_ManualDriving` 3 s later and then back to `TS_Parked`. A new timetable with the autopilot on does nothing: no docking events and no movement for 4 minutes.
  - `AFGTrain::CancelDockingSequence()` releases it. `IsDockingCancelRequested()` turns 1, and `TDS_None` follows on the platform's next check: after 2.5 s in `ETPDS_WaitForTransferCondition`, 0.2 s in `ETPDS_IdleWaitForTime`. After that a new order works.
  - **`CancelDockingSequence()` does not end a docking whose platform is in the load/unload phase.** A test emptied the docked wagons into their unloading platforms by code at the docking frame (the platforms were already `Unloading`) and then called `CancelDockingSequence()` on the train every 3 s: the train stayed `TDS_Docked` until the phase timer ran out, `Unloading -> WaitingForTransfer after 27.0 s`, and was docked for 27.0 s each time (6 dockings, one and two wagons). Cancelling works for a platform in `ETPDS_WaitForTransferCondition`, not in `ETPDS_Loading`/`ETPDS_Unloading`. Moving the cargo yourself therefore does not shorten a docking; shortening the platform's timer does (see "The 27 s load/unload phase is a per-platform timer"). Evidence: log lines `cancelled the docking of <train>, 27.0 s after it docked`, `docked for 27.0 s`. (2026-10-05, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **`TDS_ReadyToDock` lasts as long as a player sits in the cab of a train standing on a station, or a train with the autopilot on and no stops stands on one.** `CancelDockingSequence` does not end it, and it does not block a new timetable: writing stops and switching the autopilot on drives the train away normally. Only `TDS_Docked` needs the cancel before a new timetable works. (2026-10-04, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- `ETrainDockingState` of a train that stood at a station as `TDS_ReadyToDock` falls back to `TDS_None` the moment the train drives away from that station, not only when a docking finishes. Code that takes "`TDS_None` after I started a move" as "the move is done" ends the move a second after it began; check that the train is under its destination station as well. Seen as a move command re-issued every 10 s while the train stood at another station, with every other check green. Evidence: `<Agent>: move <train> to <station A>` every cycle while the status line read `at <station B>`. (2026-10-06, game 502094, SML 3.12.0, UE 5.6.1-CSS)
- **A timetable with a single stop (load once, filter "none", 1 s) leaves the train docked at that station for good.** Give a circulating test train at least two stops. (2026-10-04, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **An order written to a train standing at station X, with X as its second stop:** `SDLE_NoPath` for 1.9 s, then `SDLE_NoError`, a brief `TDS_ReadyToDock` → `TDS_None` at X, and the train drives off normally.
- **The game's own error for a route that disappears under a running timetable.** A track on the way to the next stop was dismantled from code (`IFGDismantleInterface::Execute_Dismantle`) while the train was docked at the stop before; when the docking ended the train reported `SDLE_StationUnreachable` and stood with the autopilot on. Dismantling the four one-way signals of a passing loop the same way made both loop tracks two-way for the route search at once. Evidence: `<Mod>: job … ends: ESelfDrivingLocomotiveError::SDLE_StationUnreachable` 6 s after the transfer ended. (2026-10-05, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **An unreachable next station:** for example, a track piece is missing. The train reports `SDLE_StationUnreachable` right after the previous docking ends and waits with the autopilot on. It retries by itself: once the track and power were rebuilt, the error went through `SDLE_NoPower` to `SDLE_NoError` and the train drove on.
- **The game does not dock a train that stands on a station's track past its docking point.** A train whose autopilot run was called off a second after it set off stopped about a metre past the station's docking point. A timetable to that same station made the autopilot take the train as arrived: it never docked and never moved, and trains queued behind it waited for good. A route query from that spot to the station is answered as reachable, so a mod must treat such a station as unreachable from there and send the train elsewhere (a lap round) or to another place. Telling it apart: record the front vehicle's track and offset when the train docks (`TDS_Docked`); a train without a run counts as at the station only within a few dozen centimetres of that spot. A docking that ends within one collect pass is missed by a per-second poll, so record it where docking is handled. (2026-10-07, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **`AFGTrain::OnCollided` derails a moving train from code.** Calling `Train->OnCollided(Train->GetFirstVehicle(), Train->mSimulationData.Velocity, Other->GetFirstVehicle(), 0.f, true)` on a train running on the autopilot, with another train's first vehicle, set `IsDerailed()` within a second; the autopilot run ended at once. Useful to test derailment handling without building a crash. (2026-10-07, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **Travel times of the autopilot repeat to a tenth of a second.** The same train on the same free route took 35.5 s five times out of five (559.5 m, loaded) and 40.2–40.3 s four times (648.1 m, empty). The same delivery took 102.8 s four times. So a time measured once already predicts the next drive, as long as no other train or signal holds it up. The first prediction, from the route length and a default 15 m/s, was 10% short. Evidence: `drove <station A> -> <station B> in 35.5 s, 559.5 m, 15.75 m/s, mass 400.0 t`, five lines. (2026-10-06, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **A train that passes a station without stopping slows down through it.** A locomotive with one Freight Car on a one-way loop drove 53 km/h before a station it did not stop at, 47 km/h on the station's track and 82 km/h after it, the same on four passes. The cause (station track, curve or a signal at the station) was not checked. **[?]** A tester driving by hand sees trains slow down before path signals as normal game behaviour, so a path signal at the station is the likely cause; not measured with and without one. Evidence: `Train through <station C> (pass 1): 53 km/h before, 47 entering, lowest 47, 82 leaving`, passes 2–4 identical. (2026-10-06, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- Holding a train still so that nobody drives it: autopilot on and an empty timetable (`TimeTable->SetStops({})`, then `SetSelfDrivingEnabled(true)`). The train stands at 0 km/h with `SDLE_NoTimeTable`, and the player cannot drive it from the cab. To release it, write the stops back, then switch the autopilot off and on so it picks up the new stop. If the player switches the autopilot off meanwhile, put it back on in the mod's next collect (a "relock").
