# Railways (domain)

Part of the `satisfactory-modding` skill. Open this domain only when the mod deals with trains, timetables, stations, platforms, tracks, signals or routes. Markers: no marker = verified in practice, **[source]** = read from SML or game code, not tested, **[?]** = uncertain. Replace the parts in `<...>` with your own.

Most of this domain was first read from game headers (changelist 491125, later checked against 502094) and asset stubs. The parts without a marker were tested in the packaged game 502094 with SML 3.12.0 on a single player host: a test scenario wrote timetables from code and logged the train's delegates next to its state polled every 0.1 s. Check names against the current headers (see `patterns-and-pitfalls.md`).

## Pitfalls

- **Other mods that drive trains.** Dynamic Train Routes (mod reference `DynamicTrainRoutes`, tested 1.3.2) and `DynamicTimeTable` (tested 1.4.2) write timetables and routes themselves. A mod that also writes them can detect them at start with `UModLoadingLibrary::IsModLoaded` (`references/sml-api.md`) and stand aside: with both loaded, the tested mod logged `<Mod> is inactive: DynamicTrainRoutes, DynamicTimeTable loaded`, ran 0 cycles in 5 s and refused a change from its window, in 2 runs. (2026-10-04, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **Dynamic Train Routes 1.3.3 hooks only `AFGTrain::FindPath`, not `FRailroadNavigation::FindPathSync`.** The log shows one line `Successfully hooked function AFGTrain::FindPath` for it and none for `FindPathSync`. So a train's autopilot gets DTR's penalised route (a standing train ahead sends it the long way round at once), while a mod's own `FindPathSync(Locomotive, Station)` call next to it still returns the game's plain shortest route: in one run the autopilot path was 17 points, 985 m via the detour and the mod's own search 16 points, 736 m via the blocked block. DTR writes its route again about every 2 s (`RepathInterval=2` in `FactoryGame/Configs/DynamicTrainRoutes.cfg`, default penalties `StationPenalty=100`, `OncomingTrainPenalty=200`, idle-train penalty up to 4000 after 90 s), so a route a mod writes into `mAtcData.Path` is replaced by DTR's within 2 s while the train is moving, and the mod has to write it again each cycle to keep it (81 replacements in one parking test of 19 minutes, 5 rewrites in 8 s before one switch). The replacement shows up as the game's path object changing between two ticks; the outcome (where the train goes) is decided by whoever writes last before the switch or signal. The mod's log category `LogDynamicTrainPathfinding` prints nothing at default verbosity. (2026-11-06, game 517554, SML 3.12.0, DTR 1.3.3)
- **DynamicTimeTable 1.4.2 hooks `AFGRailroadTimeTable::SetStops` and swaps a stop inside that call.** When a timetable written by another mod has a stop that is a station of a DTT group, and that station is taken (default capacity 1 per station, counted on all trains whose timetable names it), DTT replaces the stop with another station of the same group before the write lands: the mod wrote `[<station V>, <station Z1>]`, the timetable read back the same tick was `[<station V>, <station Z2>]`. A group that appears twice in the timetable (two of its stops are group stations) is left alone, as the 1.3.1 source says. DTT logs nothing about the swap even with `-LogCmds="LogDTTSubsystem Verbose"`; its start lines are `LogDTTSubsystem: DynamicTimeTable subsystem initialized.`, `Created new DynamicTimeTable save-data actor with an empty configuration.`, `Registered chat command: /dtt`, `LogDTTModule: dTT RCO registration. Success=true`. (2026-11-06, game 517554, SML 3.12.0, DTT 1.4.2)
- **DynamicTimeTable 1.4.2 groups can be made from code through its RCO, by reflection.** Classes: `DTTSubsystem` (world actor; `CreateGroup(GroupName)->FGuid`, `GetStationGroups()`, `GetGroupRuntimeLoad(GroupID, OutLoads)`, `GetAllVanillaStations(OutStations)`, `RecalculateBookingsFromVanilla()`), `DTTRemoteCallObject` (one per player; `Server_RenameGroup`, `Server_AddStation(GroupID: FGuid, StationId: FString, RequestID: int32, ExpectedRevision: int32, ClientProtocol: int32)`, `Server_SetStationCapacity(StationId, Capacity, RequestID, ExpectedRevision, ClientProtocol)`, `Server_RemoveStation`, `Server_DeleteGroup`), `DTTClientUIControllerSubsystem`, `DTTSaveDataActor`. A call is accepted only with `ClientProtocol` equal to the version in the RCO's `CachedSnapshot` property (`ProtocolVersion=35` in 1.4.2, read with `ExportText`), `RequestID > 0` (0 is logged as `INVALID_MUTATION_REQUEST_ID Request=0`), `ExpectedRevision` equal to the current configuration revision (2 right after `CreateGroup`, +1 for every accepted change) and `StationId` the station identifier's object path without the class prefix (`/Game/FactoryGame/Map/GameLevel01/Persistent_Level.Persistent_Level:PersistentLevel.FGTrainStationIdentifier_NNN`, the form of `DTTSaveDataActor::SavedStationSettings`). A refused call is silent otherwise; the only way to see acceptance is the snapshot property changing on the next tick. Setting a capacity to 1 is a no-op, because 1 is the default. (2026-11-06, game 517554, SML 3.12.0, DTT 1.4.2)
- **DynamicTimeTable 1.4.2 counts a station as taken by every train whose timetable names it, wherever that train is.** With a group {<station Z1>, <station Z2>} of capacity 1 each and a player's train whose timetable held <station Z1> all the time, every timetable another mod wrote with <station Z1> once was swapped to <station Z2> inside `SetStops`: 12 of 12 writes in 25 minutes, one every 121 s, in two runs (one with Dynamic Train Routes loaded too). DTT moved the player's train to the other station only in an earlier run where the dispatcher's train itself drove on the swapped timetable and changed the bookings. So a dispatcher mod cannot deliver to a group station of capacity 1 that a player's timetable names; it can only detect the swap and give the job up. (2026-11-06, game 517554, SML 3.12.0, DTT 1.4.2)
- **The swap happens inside the `SetStops` call, so reading the stops back right after writing them (`GetStops`) shows it in the same tick**, before the train moves; a check in a later cycle sees the same thing a second later. The swapped timetable keeps the number of stops and replaces one stop's station. (2026-11-06, game 517554, SML 3.12.0, DTT 1.4.2)
- **DTT's `UDTTSubsystem` is a `UWorldSubsystem` outered to the world** (`<map>:Persistent_Level.Persistent_Level:DTTSubsystem_NNN`, not under `PersistentLevel`), not an actor: `UGameplayStatics::GetActorOfClass` finds nothing. Found by class name with `FindFirstObject<UClass>(TEXT("DTTSubsystem"), EFindFirstObjectOptions::NativeFirst)` and a `TObjectIterator<UObject>` filtered by `IsA(Class)`, `!IsTemplate()` and `GetWorld() == <world>`, once per world. Its `GetStationGroups()` (BlueprintCallable, one return parameter `TArray<struct>` with `GroupID: FGuid`, `GroupName: FString`, `Stations: TArray<struct{StationIdentifier: AFGTrainStationIdentifier*, StationId: FString}>`) is read without any dependency on the module: `FindFunction`, a parameter buffer of `ParmsSize` with the return property initialised (`InitializeValue_InContainer`), `ProcessEvent`, then `FScriptArrayHelper` over the return `FArrayProperty`, `FStructProperty::Struct->FindPropertyByName` for the fields, `FObjectProperty::GetObjectPropertyValue_InContainer` for the station, and `DestroyValue_InContainer` at the end (the array's count is gone once it is destroyed). A read every 5 s plus one on demand is cheap. (2026-11-06, game 517554, SML 3.12.0, DTT 1.4.2)
- **Dynamic Train Routes 1.3.3 and DynamicTimeTable 1.4.2 loaded together with a dispatcher mod that keeps working (no inactive mode) left its control scenarios unchanged**: a parking cycle of 19 minutes took 1146 s against 1143 s without the mods, a two-way single-track scenario 111 s in both, and the station tab, signal window and train switch worked as without them, in one run each. (2026-11-06, game 517554, SML 3.12.0)
- **[source]** Class names suggested by a chatbot, `AFGBuildableRailroadTrackConnection` and `AFGTrainHalt`, do not exist. The right ones are `UFGRailroadTrackConnectionComponent`, `AFGBuildableRailroadStation` and `AFGTrainStationIdentifier`. Always verify game class names in the headers.
- Solid-cargo belts and pipes keep running while a platform waits for its transfer condition and stand still in its 27 s load/unload phase (tested in game 513885 for belts, 502094 for pipes; the mod Uninterrupted Train Belts/Pipes changes that, see `domains/railroad/timetable-docking.md`, "Docking rules in practice").
- **A track hologram's free end takes its direction from where the player looks.** A track started on a free track end and ended on the floor (no end to snap to) gets its end tangent from the player's view, so a line of straight pieces built from code with the player looking down came out as a zigzag (the user's screenshot; later pieces refused with `FGCDTrackTrunToSharp` "Railroad Track turns too sharply!"). Turning the player to look along the piece before each click (from behind its start, along its direction) made every piece straight and joined. Evidence: <scenario>, 8 pieces with the lengths of the description in the layout dump, no "too sharply". (2026-10-25, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- **[?] A freight wagon placed after a Buffer Stop at the end of its platform's track is refused** with `FGCDNotEnoughSpaceOnTrack` "Not enough space on track!", the locomotive on the station in front of it placed fine; in a hand-built copy of the same layout the wagon stands there with the Buffer Stop. Not checked yet: placing the train before the Buffer Stop. (2026-10-25, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
