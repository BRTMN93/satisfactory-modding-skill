# Railways: autopilot speed limits, braking and ATC data

Part of the `satisfactory-modding` skill, railroad domain (index: `domains/railroad/README.md`). Split from `domains/railroad/route-autopilot.md`, which holds what the autopilot does with a route. Markers: no marker = verified in practice, **[source]** = read from SML or game code, not tested, **[?]** = uncertain.

## Speed limits, braking and ATC data

- **A train's speed can be limited from code without a hook: write the rated maximum of every vehicle and recalculate the consist.** `UFGRailroadVehicleMovementComponent::mMaxVelocity` (km/h, a protected `EditAnywhere` UPROPERTY, written by reflection: `FindFProperty<FFloatProperty>(Movement->GetClass(), TEXT("mMaxVelocity"))`) on each vehicle of the train, then `AFGTrain::RecalculateConsistData()`: `mConsistData.MaxSpeed` and `MaxBrakeDistance` follow, and the autopilot's cruise target becomes the new maximum.
  - A locomotive with a Freight Car (330 t, rated 200/200 km/h, driving at most 120 km/h) set to 40 km/h held 36.5-39.6 km/h over 533 m of each 640 m section (98-100% of the per-frame samples within -3/+2 km/h), kept it after the autopilot was switched off and on (a new route of the game) and after a 1 s docking at the station in the middle, and nothing put the values back (0 resets in 159 s). The autopilot approaches the target from below like a proportional controller: throttle 0.20 at 0 km/h, 0.01 at 39 km/h, 0.00 at 39.6 km/h. The braking for the next docking then starts about 18 m before its 29 m restricted zone (`MaxBrakeDistance` of 40 km/h = 18.5 m) with the air brake, and the train docked normally twice. The values are not saved (a load brings the class defaults back), so a mod writes them again after every load.
  - Evidence: `<scenario> vehicle at 40 km/h: docked 2 of 2, autopilot errors 0, the game's routes 3, writes 0, put back by the game 0; section 1 to <station B>: held 40 km/h over 533 m for 49.1 s, 98.0% of 2945 samples within -3/+2 km/h, lowest 36.5, highest 39.6 ...; section 2 ... 100.0% of 2918 samples ... lowest 37.0, highest 39.6`. (2026-10-28, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- **`mConsistData.MaxSpeed` alone is not enough: the braking curve towards a station or a signal is not clamped by it.** With `MaxSpeed` written to 40 km/h (cm/s) and nothing else, the train held 37-39.7 km/h on the open line (the game never put the value back, also not at a route change or a docking), but as soon as the dock point came within the consist's `MaxBrakeDistance` (458 m, still the value for the rated 200 km/h) the target jumped to 192.8 km/h, the braking curve of the dock point, and the train sprinted to 113-118 km/h before braking for the station. Evidence: `t=25.0 <train>: 39.1 km/h, target 40.0 ... restrictive RSA_None`, `t=26.0 <train>: 39.7 km/h, target 192.8 ... restrictive RSA_Dock 458 m`, section results `35.0% of 1595 samples within -3/+2 km/h, lowest 37.0, highest 113.3`. (2026-10-28, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- **`FTrainAtcPoint::SpeedLimit` is dead data for the autopilot, and `mAtcData.TargetSpeed` is recomputed every tick.** The game leaves `SpeedLimit` at 0 on every target point, the dock goal included; 40 km/h written into every point from a core ticker every frame (69 writes) changed nothing (the target stayed 200 km/h, the train reached 120 km/h), and the game builds a fresh array with zeros whenever the train passes a point (54 of 56 values gone that way, 2 overwritten in place). `TargetSpeed` written every frame (4174 writes) changed nothing either: the throttle was 1.00 at 116.7 km/h with 40 in the field, so the game derives the throttle from the target it computed itself earlier in the tick. Evidence: `<scenario> points at 40 km/h: ... writes 69, points put back by the game 56 (54 of them in a fresh array), fresh arrays 16; ... highest 120.0`, `<scenario> target at 40 km/h: ... writes 4174 ... highest 120.3`. (2026-10-28, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- **What the autopilot's target speed is, measured.** With nothing restricting: the consist's `MaxSpeed` (the rated maximum, 200 km/h for an Electric Locomotive with a Freight Car; the train drives at most about 120 km/h, by its tractive effort). A restrictive point (a station to dock at, a signal at stop) counts once it is within the consist's `MaxBrakeDistance` (463 m = 55.6² / (2 · 3.33) from the rated 200 km/h; seen at 459 m and 458 m, not at 515 m); from then on the target is the braking curve `sqrt(v_end² + 2 · a · (d − 29 m))` with `a` = `MaxBrakeDeceleration` (3.33 m/s² for that train) and `v_end` = 15 km/h for a docking (the restricted speed of the last 29 m before the stop point, stop offset 8 m): 94.8 km/h at 131 m, 131.2 km/h at 227 m, 185.8 km/h at 427 m. 0 to 120 km/h takes about 15 s and 250 m; 120 km/h to the dock about 10 s and 170 m with the air brake (`LastSpeedControl` -1). Evidence: trace lines `<train>: 118.3 km/h, target 185.8, brake 162 m ... restrictive RSA_Dock 427 m`, `99.0 km/h, target 94.8 ... air 1.00 ... RSA_Dock 131 m`, `14.5 km/h, target 15.0 ... RSA_Dock 13 m`. (2026-10-28, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
  - **[?]** Other braking numbers in this domain: 152 m, the start of braking of a light train (locomotive and two empty Freight Cars) from 120 km/h for a red block signal (`domains/railroad/signal-placement.md`, "Block validation and path signals"), and about 3.6 m/s² as the `BrakeDistance` of a train braking from 111 km/h (`domains/railroad/own-routes.md`, "A route can be changed under a moving train in both directions with `SetPath`"). These three numbers (463 m with 3.33 m/s² here, 152 m, 3.6 m/s²) come from different runs and are not reconciled.
- **A speed limit set through the vehicles' rated maximum is not saved, can be put back on at once after a load, and can be lifted on the way.** A train saved at 39.6 km/h with its vehicles' `mMaxVelocity` at 40 came back from the load (the same process, through the main menu) at 39.5 km/h with the class defaults again (`mMaxVelocity` 200/200, `mConsistData.MaxSpeed` 200 km/h) and the game's own route already found; writing the values again in the frame the train was found held it at the limit for the rest of the drive. In the first second after the load the game itself cut the speed from 39.5 to 33.4 km/h (`SDLE_NoPower` for 0.4 s, the same dip as 95 to 61 km/h in a load test without any limit), and the proportional control took 5 s to get back above 37 km/h and 15 s to 39.3. Lifting the limit on the way (the class defaults written back, `RecalculateConsistData()` at 39.5 km/h) let the train speed up to 113 km/h and dock normally; at 80 km/h the same mechanism held 77.0-79.5 km/h over 423 m. Evidence: `loaded <save>: <train> at 39.5 km/h (saved at 39.6), consist max 200.0 km/h, vehicles 200/200 km/h ... route yes`, `mMaxVelocity 200/200 -> 40/40 km/h written again after the load`, `the limit lifted at 39.5 km/h ... MaxSpeed 200.0 km/h`, `section 2 ... highest 113.2 ... docked 1`, `held 80 km/h over 423 m for 19.3 s, 100.0% of 1157 samples`. (2026-10-28, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- **`mAtcData.NextSignalConnection` is the nearest signal within the autopilot's search distance, not the first signal on the train's route; to know which signal comes first, the route has to be walked.** `FTrainAtcData::UpdateNextSignal(searchDistance)` keeps "the closest signal within the search distance" (`FGTrain.h`).
  - On a 650 m stretch with no signal between a train and the path signal at its end, a check through `NextSignalConnection` saw that path signal only about 400-460 m before it (train limited to 85-90 km/h), while a walk over `mAtcData.Path->PathPoints` from `max(CurrentPathSegment, 0)`, taking the first point whose `TrackConnection->GetFacingSignal()` is set, found it as soon as the train had passed the signal before it (about 600 m before it), in 2 of 2 runs each. An unlimited train (rated 200 km/h) saw a path signal 272 m ahead through `NextSignalConnection`. The game's path reservation does not wait for `NextSignalConnection` (see the reservation queue in `domains/railroad/scheduler.md`), so a mod's decision that "the path signal is next, the way is asked for" has to come from the route walk.
  - Evidence: `Driver: train <train> does not yield to <other>: the junction's path signal is its next signal` logged with `<train>` about 460 m from the junction with the `NextSignalConnection` check and about 650 m with the route walk, same layout and start times. **[?]** The search distance seems to follow the consist's `MaxBrakeDistance` (a train limited to 40 km/h, 18.5 m, appeared to ask for its path only some tens of metres before its path signal); not measured directly. (2026-11-02, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- **The rated-maximum speed limit works on a dedicated server, and its clients see it without writing anything.** Written on the server (`mMaxVelocity` of every vehicle by reflection, then `AFGTrain::RecalculateConsistData()`), a limit of 40 km/h held 37.0-39.6 km/h over 532 m of each 640 m section (100% of the server's samples within -3/+2 km/h) and the train docked twice, as on a host. A game joined as a client measured the train from the replicated position of its leading vehicle: 1294 m driven, at most 39.9 km/h (the vehicle's replicated forward speed at most 39.6 km/h), with no write on the client. Evidence: server `<scenario> vehicle at 40 km/h: docked 2 of 2, autopilot errors 0 ...; section 1 to <station B>: held 40 km/h over 532 m for 48.7 s, 100.0% of 1045 samples within -3/+2 km/h, lowest 37.0, highest 39.6 ...`, client `client: <train> moved 1294 m, highest replicated speed 39.9 km/h (vehicle 39.6) against the limit 40 km/h, stood 20 s at the end`. (2026-10-28, game and dedicated server 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- **The autopilot's distances run from the middle of the leading vehicle, not from its front.** A train held natively before a block signal at stop has `mAtcData.NextRestrictiveSignalDistance` 10.0 m (the stop offset), while its front taken from world positions (actor location + half the vehicle's `GetLength()`) stands 2.0 m before the signal's joint; a train whose route ends at a signal (stop offset 8 m) stands with that front exactly on the joint. So "stops 10.0 m before a signal" is the autopilot's measure, about 2 m physically for a locomotive 16 m long, and the physical gap to another train from `CurrentConnectionDistance` or from route points is the measured distance less half the leading vehicle. **[?]** The cause cannot be told apart from an actor location that is not the vehicle's middle; the physical gap from world positions and the autopilot's own measure do not depend on which. Evidence: `<scenario> F held: F stands 2.0 m before E (E at stop, L in the block), its next restrictive point 10.0 m E` (5 runs), the leader on a route cut at the next signal `front 768.0 m` with the block 768.0 m long. (2026-11-03, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- **[source]** What `AFGTrain::TickAtc` does, read from the game's disassembly (exported, own body, called with `call`, server only, once a frame per train from `AFGRailroadSubsystem::Tick` before `TickSelfDriving`, the scheduler and the movement): advance and refresh the target points (a point's aspect is read straight from the facing signal's `mAspect`), `TargetSpeed = mConsistData.MaxSpeed`, `UpdateNextRestrictiveSignal(MaxBrakeDistance)` (the first point at stop or dock within that window, so the window does follow `MaxBrakeDistance`), `UpdateNextSignal`, `UpdateNextSwitch(150 m)`, then the braking curve `sqrt(v_r² + 2·max(0, a − G·grade/M)·(d − 30 m))` with 15 km/h on the last 30 m and 0 below the stop offset, `TargetSpeed = 0` without power. `TickSelfDriving_FollowPath` then reads `TargetSpeed` and the `NextRestrictiveSignal*` fields: it arms the movement's catch (`mSimulationData.CatchAtConnectionDistance = StopOffset`) when the restrictive connection is `CurrentConnection` and `StopOffset + 4 m > Distance`, brakes fully at a target of 0, and the scheduler reads only `NextSignal*`, `BrakeDistance` and the signals, never these fields. (2026-11-03, game 513885, SML 3.12.0, disassembly)
- **A train's ATC data lags its block occupancy by up to one frame: code that runs after the railroad subsystem's tick sees a train already counted in the block ahead (`FFGRailroadSignalBlock::IsOccupiedBy`) while `mAtcData.CurrentConnection` still names the track short of the signal.** `TickAtc` refreshes `mAtcData` before the frame's move, the move then carries the front past the signal and updates the block occupancy. A hook after `TickAtc` that records "the front is in the block" from `mAtcData.CurrentConnection` is therefore one frame late for any decision taken in a later tick of the same frame; `Block->IsOccupiedBy(Train)` is current there. Evidence: `<scenario>` with a reason to stop forced in that frame: with the ATC test the trigger never fired (1 entry at 119 km/h, `no result in 480 s`); with the occupancy test it fired in the entry frame, and without the guard the train was let go onto native signalling and ran into `<train>` standing ahead (`contact: 0.48 m at 120.5 km/h`); in normal runs the occupancy guard acted at every armed entry (`taken as inside`, 0 losses). (2026-11-10, game 517554, SML 3.12.0)
- **The rated-maximum speed limit also stops and holds a train without a hook: written as 0.01 km/h, the autopilot brakes to a stop and stands until the limit is lifted.** The same writing as for a speed limit (`mMaxVelocity` of every vehicle by reflection, then `AFGTrain::RecalculateConsistData()`), with 0.01 km/h, on a train driving at 30.7 km/h one second after a load: the train stopped in the block behind <train> and stood (0.0 km/h 61 s later), then, with its own limit written back, drove on along its route past the next signal. Evidence: `held still after the load: 1.00 s after the first frame of the loaded world, <train> 30.7 km/h, gap 42.5 m`, `may go, <leader> out of the block, <train> 0.0 km/h`, smallest gap 41.6 m, no contact, in 2 runs. (2026-11-03, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- **[?]** A train whose vehicles' `mMaxVelocity` was written down to 20 km/h for the whole leg to a station stopped at 0 km/h for good without docking: `TDS_None`, `SDLE_NoError`, `mSelfDrivingData.TimeWaitingAtSignal` 0, autopilot on, for over 5 minutes until the scenario ended, in two runs of one layout, with and without another mod rewriting routes. The same limit on routes that end at a signal parks the train normally, and a limit written only after the train left its station did not hinder the stop at a signal. Whether the train stands at the stop point or short of it, and why, is not known: not read from the code and not tried at other speeds or stations. Evidence: two runs of `<scenario>` with the limit from the start, the train reached the end of the leg at about t=480-510 s and stood there until the stage limit. (2026-11-06, game 517554, SML 3.12.0)
- **A train on the autopilot whose restrictive point jumps back to a stop signal about 60 m ahead at line speed stops natively before that signal, hard but without passing it.** A follower whose ATC fields had been overwritten to drive past a block signal at stop (a hook before `AFGTrain::TickAtc`) got its native supervision back at 116.6 km/h with its front 59.9 m before the signal (the overwriting turned off, so the game's own restrictive point at the signal came back): it stopped after 57.4 m, about 9.1 m/s² on average, 2.0 m before the signal's joint, the signal at stop and the ATC's distance to it 10.0 m. The same twice (setting turned off; an invariant broken at 116.6 km/h 59.8 m before). Far above the 3.33 m/s² of the braking curve (`MaxBrakeDeceleration`): the autopilot brakes harder than its curve when the stop point comes inside the curve's distance. Evidence: `<scenario> result: far offfar at 116.6 km/h 59.9 m before E; F stopped after 57.4 m (about 9.13 m/s2), 2.0 m before E (its nearest end), E at stop, 10.0 m ahead by the ATC, ... losses 0`. (2026-11-03, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
