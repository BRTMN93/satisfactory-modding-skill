# Railways: driving a train through its timetable

Part of the `satisfactory-modding` skill, railroad domain (index: `domains/railroad/README.md`); the timetable and docking rules are in `domains/railroad/timetable-docking.md`. Markers: no marker = verified in practice, **[source]** = read from SML or game code, not tested, **[?]** = uncertain. Replace the parts in `<...>` with your own.

## Driving a train through its timetable

This works without hooks:

```cpp
AFGRailroadTimeTable* TimeTable = Train->GetTimeTable();
if (!TimeTable) TimeTable = Train->NewTimeTable();      // a train that never had a timetable returns nullptr
TArray<FTimeTableStop> Stops;
FTimeTableStop& Load = Stops.AddDefaulted_GetRef();
Load.Station = SupplierIdentifier;
Load.DockingRuleSet.DockingDefinition = ETrainDockingDefinition::TDD_FullyLoadUnload;  // still "or 15 s", see above
Load.DockingRuleSet.LoadFilterDescriptors.Add(ItemClass);
// ... the unloading stop the same way, with UnloadFilterDescriptors
TimeTable->SetStops(Stops);
TimeTable->SetCurrentStop(0);
Train->SetSelfDrivingEnabled(true);
```

- **Without stops:** with no timetable, or with 0 stops, `SetSelfDrivingEnabled(true)` broadcasts the autopilot and `TS_SelfDriving`. Within about 0.3 s `SDLE_NoTimeTable` follows. The AI stays `SDLS_Idle` and the train does not move.
  - **This holds a standing train still and locked for the player.** `SetSelfDrivingEnabled(true)` on a train with an empty timetable gives `SDLE_NoTimeTable`: the train brakes to a stop and stays, also on a station, and the game takes no input from the cab (the player pressed throttle in the cab for 40 s, 0 km/h). The game itself does not switch such an autopilot off. A mod can use this to keep a train out of the player's hands without any hook. The locomotive window of such a train shows the game's own warning "Locomotive has no Timetable." (screenshot, 2026-10-05). (2026-10-04, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **After `SetStops`, `SetCurrentStop(0)` and autopilot on:** on the next tick the error clears to `SDLE_NoError`, the AI goes to `SDLS_FollowPath` and `mAtcData.Path` is set. Turning the autopilot off and on in the same frame broadcasts off/on and `TS_Parked`/`TS_SelfDriving` each time.
- **Arrival, all in one frame:**
  1. `mOnDockingStateChanged(TDS_ReadyToDock)`,
  2. `mOnDockingStateChanged(TDS_Docked)`,
  3. the current stop already moves to the **next** stop (0 → 1), the path is cleared, the AI is `SDLS_Docking` and the platform is `ETPDS_Loading` or `ETPDS_WaitForTransferCondition`.
- **When the `TDS_Docked` delegate fires, `mDockedAtStation` is still null.** It is set later in the same frame. To know the station inside the delegate, remember `mAtcData.Path->Station` before docking.
- **End of the transfer:** `mOnDockingStateChanged(TDS_None)`. Then a brief `TDS_ReadyToDock` → `TDS_None` as the train looks for the path to the next stop, `mDockedAtStation` becomes null, and the AI goes back to `SDLS_FollowPath`.
- **Timing:** loading or unloading one wagon took 27–30 s from `TDS_Docked` to `TDS_None`.
- **Stopping after the last stop works from inside the `TDS_None` handler:** call `SetSelfDrivingEnabled(false)` and `SetStops({})`.
  - The train stays at the station: 0 m moved in 15 s, status `TS_Parked`, AI `SDLS_DockingCompleted`, platform `ETPDS_None`.
  - A new order from that state finds its path at once and the train leaves. Because it stands at a station, a brief `TDS_ReadyToDock` → `TDS_None` comes first.
- **An order whose first stop is the station the train stands at is served in place.** `TDS_ReadyToDock`, `TDS_Docked` and `TDS_None` came in the frame after the autopilot was turned on, with 0 m driven. Tested with an empty wagon on a full-unload stop, and with a full wagon, which was unloaded in place (a scenario's PASS, 2026-10-04, game 502094, SML 3.12.0).
- **A docking that cannot finish is not released by turning the autopilot off and clearing the timetable.** The train stays `TDS_Docked` and the platform stays in `ETPDS_WaitForTransferCondition`. The game itself switches the status to `TS_ManualDriving` 3 s later and then back to `TS_Parked`. A new timetable with the autopilot on does nothing: no docking events and no movement for 4 minutes.
  - `AFGTrain::CancelDockingSequence()` releases it. `IsDockingCancelRequested()` turns 1, and `TDS_None` follows on the platform's next check: after 2.5 s in `ETPDS_WaitForTransferCondition`, 0.2 s in `ETPDS_IdleWaitForTime`. After that a new order works.
  - **`CancelDockingSequence()` does not end a docking whose platform is in the load/unload phase.** A test emptied the docked wagons into their unloading platforms by code at the docking frame (the platforms were already `Unloading`) and then called `CancelDockingSequence()` on the train every 3 s: the train stayed `TDS_Docked` until the phase timer ran out, `Unloading -> WaitingForTransfer after 27.0 s`, and was docked for 27.0 s each time (6 dockings, one and two wagons). Cancelling works for a platform in `ETPDS_WaitForTransferCondition`, not in `ETPDS_Loading`/`ETPDS_Unloading`. Moving the cargo yourself therefore does not shorten a docking; shortening the platform's timer does (see "The 27 s load/unload phase is a per-platform timer"). Evidence: log lines `cancelled the docking of <train>, 27.0 s after it docked`, `docked for 27.0 s`. (2026-10-05, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **`TDS_ReadyToDock` lasts as long as a player sits in the cab of a train standing on a station, or a train with the autopilot on and no stops stands on one.** `CancelDockingSequence` does not end it, and it does not block a new timetable: writing stops and switching the autopilot on drives the train away normally. Only `TDS_Docked` needs the cancel before a new timetable works. (2026-10-04, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- `ETrainDockingState` of a train that stood at a station as `TDS_ReadyToDock` falls back to `TDS_None` the moment the train drives away from that station, not only when a docking finishes. Code that takes "`TDS_None` after I started a move" as "the move is done" ends the move a second after it began; check that the train is under its destination station as well. Seen as a move command re-issued every 10 s while the train stood at another station, with every other check green. Evidence: `<Agent>: move <train> to <station A>` every cycle while the status line read `at <station B>`. (2026-10-06, game 502094, SML 3.12.0, UE 5.6.1-CSS)
- **A timetable with a single stop (load once, filter "none", 1 s) leaves the train docked at that station for good.** Give a circulating test train at least two stops. (2026-10-04, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **An order written to a train standing at station X, with X as its second stop:** `SDLE_NoPath` for 1.9 s, then `SDLE_NoError`, a brief `TDS_ReadyToDock` → `TDS_None` at X, and the train drives off normally.
- **The game's own error for a route that disappears under a running timetable.** A track on the way to the next stop was dismantled from code (`IFGDismantleInterface::Execute_Dismantle`) while the train was docked at the stop before; when the docking ended the train reported `SDLE_StationUnreachable` and stood with the autopilot on. Dismantling the four one-way signals of a passing loop the same way made both loop tracks two-way for the route search at once. Evidence: `<Mod>: job … ends: ESelfDrivingLocomotiveError::SDLE_StationUnreachable` 6 s after the transfer ended. (2026-10-05, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **An unreachable next station:** for example, a track piece is missing. The train reports `SDLE_StationUnreachable` right after the previous docking ends and waits with the autopilot on. It retries by itself: once the track and power were rebuilt, the error went through `SDLE_NoPower` to `SDLE_NoError` and the train drove on.
- **The game does not dock a train that stands on a station's track past its docking point.** A train whose autopilot run was called off a second after it set off stopped about a metre past the station's docking point. A timetable to that same station made the autopilot take the train as arrived: it never docked and never moved, and trains queued behind it waited for good. A route query from that spot to the station is answered as reachable, so a mod must treat such a station as unreachable from there and send the train elsewhere (a lap round) or to another place. Telling it apart: record the front vehicle's track and offset when the train docks (`TDS_Docked`); a train without a run counts as at the station only within a few dozen centimetres of that spot. A docking that ends within one collect pass is missed by a per-second poll, so record it where docking is handled. (2026-10-07, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **`AFGTrain::OnCollided` derails a moving train from code.** Calling `Train->OnCollided(Train->GetFirstVehicle(), Train->mSimulationData.Velocity, Other->GetFirstVehicle(), 0.f, true)` on a train running on the autopilot, with another train's first vehicle, set `IsDerailed()` within a second; the autopilot run ended at once. Useful to test derailment handling without building a crash. (2026-10-07, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **Travel times of the autopilot repeat to a tenth of a second.** The same train on the same free route took 35.5 s five times out of five (559.5 m, loaded) and 40.2–40.3 s four times (648.1 m, empty). The same delivery took 102.8 s four times. So a time measured once already predicts the next drive, as long as no other train or signal holds it up. The first prediction, from the route length and a default 15 m/s, was 10% short. Evidence: `drove <station A> -> <station B> in 35.5 s, 559.5 m, 15.75 m/s, mass 400.0 t`, five lines. (2026-10-06, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **A train that passes a station without stopping slows down through it when a path signal stands at the station.** A locomotive with one Freight Car on a one-way loop drove 53 km/h before a station it did not stop at, 47 km/h on the station's track and 82 km/h after it, the same on four passes. Evidence: `Train through <station C> (pass 1): 53 km/h before, 47 entering, lowest 47, 82 leaving`, passes 2–4 identical. (2026-10-06, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
  - The path signal is the cause, not the station track: on four parallel straight lanes the same train passed a middle station at 120 km/h with no signal, with block signals 2 Foundations before and after it, and with no station at all, and braked to 47 km/h at the station with a path signal 2 Foundations after it. Evidence: `<scenario> PASS <station>: ... lowest within 100 m 47 km/h; error SDLE_NoError` against `lowest within 100 m 117 km/h` in the block signal lane. (2026-10-26, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- Holding a train still so that nobody drives it: autopilot on and an empty timetable (`TimeTable->SetStops({})`, then `SetSelfDrivingEnabled(true)`). The train stands at 0 km/h with `SDLE_NoTimeTable`, and the player cannot drive it from the cab. To release it, write the stops back, then switch the autopilot off and on so it picks up the new stop. If the player switches the autopilot off meanwhile, put it back on in the mod's next collect (a "relock"). Evidence: <scenario> on <save with sidings>: `held at the siding by the autopilot without stops: autopilot 1, 0 stops`, autopilot switched off by the test, 30 s later `autopilot 1, relocks 1, ... 0.0 km/h`. Job done 56 s after the release, unloaded whole. (2026-10-12, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
  - **Changing the stops inside the game's docking callback breaks nothing.** The hold (autopilot off, `SetStops({})`, autopilot on) written inside the train's `mOnDockingStateChanged` handler at `TDS_Docked` behaved frame for frame like the same hold run with `GetTimerManager().SetTimerForNextTick` (that timer ran in the same frame, after the callback returned): both trains held with `SDLE_NoTimeTable`, both were released after 30 s and arrived 19.6 s later, no ensure or error in the log. Evidence: `<train A> held inside the docking callback: autopilot 1, 0 stops, docking TDS_Docked` and the same line for `<train B> held in the next tick`. (2026-10-26, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- A train whose autopilot is switched off while it drives stops by itself. From 28–40 km/h it stood within 10 s, autopilot off, no stops, and stayed there. Evidence: <scenario> `switched Off on the way: ... 40 km/h`, then `stood after 10 s, autopilot 0, 0.0 km/h, stops 0`. (2026-10-12, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- A train parked on a dead-end siding that rises about 4 m along a curve does not roll back. It held the autopilot on with no stops (as above). Evidence: <scenario>: `moved 0.00 m in 205 s, front at height 64.4 m`, while another train passed the switch twice. (2026-10-12, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **Dismantling a train's vehicles from code during a run leaves the train's weak pointer stale within a second.** `IFGDismantleInterface::Execute_Dismantle` on every vehicle of a loaded train on its way made a `TWeakObjectPtr<AFGTrain>` kept for it invalid by the next collect (about 1 s); a map keyed by such pointers has to end the train's work there, or it vanishes silently. Evidence: `<Mod>: job … ends: its train is gone` 1 s after the dismantle. (2026-10-13, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- On a big save one call `AFGTrain::SetSelfDrivingEnabled(true)` (holding an idle train with the autopilot and no stops) took about 4 ms in one frame, measured as the mod's collect step (3.95 ms of a 4.08 ms frame); the same call at the same moment in two other runs of the same save cost nothing measurable. Time such game calls with the game's calls, not as the mod's own work. **[?]** The cost is likely a one-off inside the game (first autopilot setup of that train), not the mod's. Evidence: a <scenario> big-save measurement, phase 2, the frame of the line "Driver: train <train> waits for a job, held by the autopilot". (2026-10-21, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- **Throttle input from code moves no train without a controller; drive test trains with the autopilot.** `UFGLocomotiveMovementComponent::SetReverserInput(1)` and `SetThrottleInput(1)` with the autopilot off left the train standing, even with the lead locomotive set as multiple-unit master and powered; with the autopilot on (the role then reads `Disabled`) the same train drove. Evidence: `<scenario> stalled 20 s at 0.0 m`, `master set yes, role Master, power yes` (2 runs). (2026-10-25, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
  - With a controller it works: the player character put into the lead locomotive with `AFGDriveablePawn::DriverEnter` (the locomotive's controller is then the player controller), then `SetReverserInput(1)` and `SetThrottleInput(1)` every 0.1 s with the autopilot off drove the train up to 27.2 m/s, and `SetAirBrakeInput(1)` with throttle 0 stopped it. A mod that switches the autopilot back on for its own trains (a relock) has to let go of the train first, or the autopilot comes on again every tick and the train stands. Evidence: `<scenario> POSSESS ... DriverEnter 1, driver is the player 1, locomotive's controller BP_PlayerController_C_<n>`, then `t=11.4 speed 22.6 m/s`. (2026-10-26, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- A train whose autopilot is switched off while it drives at speed brakes about as hard as the autopilot's own curve, not gently: with `SetSelfDrivingEnabled(false)` at the moment its braking curve for a signal at stop began, an Electric Locomotive with one Freight Car stopped after 75.7 m from 79.5 km/h (about 3.22 m/s2, 9.9 s) and after 161.3 m from 116.3 km/h (about 3.23 m/s2, 13.0 s), each time a few metres short of the signal, with no stop or brake command of the mod. This refines the earlier "from 28-40 km/h it stood within 10 s": switching the autopilot off under a fast train does not let it roll past a red signal. Evidence: <scenario> `act result: hold at red ... at 79.5 km/h: done; stopped after 75.7 m (about 3.22 m/s2) in 9.9 s, 6.6 m before E`, and `at 116.3 km/h ... stopped after 161.3 m (about 3.23 m/s2) in 13.0 s, 4.7 m before E`. (2026-11-04, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- Reaching 120 km/h takes far more than 250 m: the same train (one locomotive, one Freight Car with 100 ore), started from a station, was at 114.5 km/h when its braking curve for a signal about 380 m ahead of the start began; with a 1088 m run-up it drove 116.3 km/h there. The last few km/h come slowly, so a train at 120 km/h takes well over 1 km of straight track. Evidence: <scenario> `not measured: F began its braking curve at 114.5 km/h` (576 m from the station to the signal), `hold at 116.3 km/h` (1088 m). (2026-11-04, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- **Uncoupling from code: `AFGRailroadSubsystem::DecoupleTrains(First, Second, Policy)` is public and decides which part keeps the train object; `AFGRailroadVehicle::DecoupleVehicleAt` and `CoupleVehicleAt` are `protected`.** On a locomotive with one Freight Car standing still, `EDecouplingPolicy::UP_Auto` (what the game's own uncoupling does) left the `AFGTrain` object with the locomotive and gave the Freight Car a new train object; `UP_KeepTrainOnSecond` left it with the Freight Car and gave the locomotive a new `AFGTrain` (a mod that keys its state by the train object then sees the locomotive as a train it never knew). Calling `DecoupleVehicleAt` from a mod does not compile (protected member). Evidence: `<scenario> re-form F: F's train object kept by the locomotive` (UP_Auto), `<scenario> re-form F: F's train object kept by the wagon; the locomotive's train <new train> (1 vehicles)` (UP_KeepTrainOnSecond). (2026-11-13, game 517554, SML 3.12.0)
