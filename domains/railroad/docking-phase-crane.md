# Railways: cargo platform phase, crane and docking edge cases

Part of the `satisfactory-modding` skill, railroad domain (index: `domains/railroad/README.md`). Split from `domains/railroad/timetable-docking.md`, which holds the timetable, the docking rules and the first measurements of the load/unload phase and the unloading crane. Markers: no marker = verified in practice, **[source]** = read from SML or game code, not tested, **[?]** = uncertain.

## Loading crane, phase timers, power cut, edge cases of a docking, dedicated server

- **The loading crane has the same timeline as the unloading one, at freight and fluid platforms alike, so the same shortening works for both directions and both kinds.** Screenshots once a second from the start of `ETPDS_Loading` at a freight platform (27 s phase): the crane grips the platform's container at 5–8 s, carries it over the wagon at 9–16 s, sets it on the wagon at 17–19 s, holds it until about 23 s and returns at 24–27 s; the cargo's look swaps at 20 s (`mTimeToSwapLoadVisibility`). A fluid station docking one wagon at a loading and one at an unloading fluid platform showed the same timeline for both (tanks lifted at 10–11 s, set down at 19–20 s, crane back at 24–27 s); both fluid platform classes have 1 skeletal mesh component and the same 27 s timers. With every cargo platform at `mTimeToCompleteLoad = mTimeToCompleteUnload = 20 + 7/1.4 = 25` and `GlobalAnimRateScale = 1.4` from 20 s into the phase (back to 1 when the status changes), ten deliveries ran 30 phases (15 loading, 15 unloading, freight) of 25.00–25.02 s each, the rate change was seen 20.02–20.03 s into each phase, and every transfer was exact (`wagon +3200, platform -3200` / `wagon -3200, platform +3200` in one frame). A platform without room for a whole wagon still took nothing in part and unloaded in 25.00 s once it had room. The empty parts of the animation can be sped up too: the crane reaches the container on the wagon (unloading) or on the platform (loading) about 4 s into the game's phase (screenshots once a second: at 3 s still above it, at 4 s on it; the unloading swap of the cargo's look is at 6 s). With the rate 1.4 from the phase's start until 4/1.4 = 2.86 s, 1.0 until 2.86 + 16 = 18.86 s and 1.4 again after that, and the timers at 23.86 s (the swap timers moved to the same animation moments, unload 4.86 s, load 18.86 s), fluid and freight phases lasted 23.86–23.87 s with the rate changes seen at 0.02, 2.88 and 18.88 s, and a viewer in full graphics found the switches smooth. Evidence: test log lines `phase 1 crane: 1.40x at 0.02 s, 1.00x at 2.88 s, 1.40x at 18.88 s` and `fluid Loading lasted 23.87 s, its timer said 23.86 s`. Evidence: test logs of 2026-10-12, lines `phase <n>: <platform> freight Loading lasted 25.00 s, its timer said 25.00 s` and `crane 1.00x -> 1.40x, 20.02 s into Loading`. (2026-10-12, game 502094, SML 3.12.0, UE 5.6.1-CSS)
- **Verified: a cargo platform's phase timers and its crane's `GlobalAnimRateScale` do not replicate; a client of a dedicated server must set both on its own copy.** The platform's docking status (`mPlatformDockingStatus`) replicates, the `mTimeToComplete*` / `mTimeToSwap*Visibility` timers and the skeletal mesh rate do not. With the timers shortened on the server only, the client saw the phase end after 23.90 s while its own copy still said 27.00 s and its crane never sped up. With the same writes done on the client (timers by reflection, rate on the platform's skeletal mesh component, switched on the replicated status changes), the client's phases lasted 23.87–23.90 s and the crane changed at 0.00, 2.87 and 18.87 s, as on the server (0.05, 2.94, 18.92 s). The client needs the server's choice of platforms: replicate it (a scope value, a platform list) rather than reading the setting on the client. Evidence: client log lines `phase 1: <station>~1 freight Loading lasted 23.90 s, its timer said 27.00 s` (without the client writes) and `phase 1 crane: 1.40x at 0.00 s, 1.00x at 2.87 s, 1.40x at 18.87 s; right 1` (with them). (2026-10-23, game 513885, SML 3.12.0, dedicated server and client on one PC)
- **The cargo of a platform transfer moves in the frame the loading or unloading phase ends, not while the status is `ETPDS_Loading`/`ETPDS_Unloading`.** A test that restocks or empties platforms only outside those two statuses still touched the platform in the frame of the transfer (`wagon changed by -3200 and the platform by 0`); restocking only while no wagon is docked (`GetDockedActor() == nullptr`) avoided it. (2026-10-23, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- A cargo platform's crane (`AFGBuildableTrainPlatformCargo`) is a `USkeletalMeshComponent` that the Blueprint adds when the actor is built (`mMagicBoxSkelMeshComponent`, `FGBuildableTrainPlatformCargo.h:213-215` **[source]**), so it exists as soon as the platform is spawned, on a client too. Changing the crane's speed through `GlobalAnimRateScale` after `Platform->GetComponents(TArray<USkeletalMeshComponent*>&)` on every change cost up to 0.25 ms a change (longest of ~46,000 frames on 71 platforms); with the mesh components found once when the platform is first seen and kept as `TWeakObjectPtr`, the longest change took 0.0004 ms. The crane pattern (1.4× while empty, 1.0× with the cargo) and the shortened phase (23.86 s instead of 27 s) are unchanged by the cache: 12 phases measured 23.86–23.87 s. Evidence: the mod's per-change timing before and after, two runs on the same large save. (2026-10-24, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- **A power cut in the middle of a load/unload phase pauses the phase and the crane.** On a save with the "No Power" game rule, `AFGGameState::SetCheatNoPower(false)` 8 s into a loading phase at a freight platform with no power grid made `HasPower()` of the platform false within a frame; after 15 s `SetCheatNoPower(true)` gave it power back. The phase (set by reflection to 23.86 s with the crane sped up after the drop, see the entries on the per-platform timer and the crane timeline) lasted 38.88 s = 23.86 s + 15.00 s: the timer did not run without power, the crane's pose did not move in the outage (0.00 change over 15 s), and the wagon was full (3200 of 3200) at the end with the platform losing what the wagon gained. Evidence: a scenario run on <test save>, log lines `phase lasted 38.88 s, platform without power from t=8.03 to t=23.03`, `the crane's pose moved 0.00 in the outage`. (2026-10-13, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **The cargo moves in the frame after the phase ends, and a belt refills the platform within a second.** At the end of a loading phase the platform goes `ETPDS_Loading -> ETPDS_WaitingForTransfer`; the wagon still reads 0 in that frame, and the cargo is in the wagon one frame later (`WaitingForTransfer -> Complete -> None`). A belt feeding the platform had put 60 items back into it 1.5 s after the transfer, so a cargo balance has to read the platform in the first frame after `WaitingForTransfer`. Evidence: `the phase is over (WaitingForTransfer), wagon 0, platform 4728`, then `status None, wagon -1, platform 1536` and 1.5 s later 60 more on the platform. (2026-10-13, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **A docking at a stop whose filter lets nothing pass ends in its frame or the next one** (seen `TDS_Docked` in frame 4803 and `TDS_None` in frame 4804, packaged game). A check for "it ends in the frame it began" fails at random; allow a few frames. (2026-10-13, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **A "fully load" docking whose wagon never fills holds the train: the platform waits in `ETPDS_IdleWaitForTime`.** A train stopped with `TDD_FullyLoadUnload` and `DockForDuration = 0` at a freight platform that a test kept nearly empty (stock taken out in lumps as it came, the wagon trimmed to 250 short of full) stood docked over 630 s. The platform's fields read by name every 15 s: status `ETPDS_IdleWaitForTime`, `mHasFullyLoadUnloadRule` 1, `mMustDockForDuration` 1, `mHasAnyRelevantStacksToMove` 1, `mAllowDepartureNoValidItemsToTransfer` 1, `mDockForDuration` 0, `mCurrentDockForDuration` counting up with the stay (601.0 at 601 s), `mTimeSinceLastLoadTransferUpdate` counting up from the last load phase (564.6 at 601 s). **[?]** One earlier run of the same setup ended the docking after 328 s with the wagon short (`TransferDone … cargo 2950`); the cause was not found and it did not come back in the run with the fields logged. Evidence: `601 s at <station A> … ETPDS_IdleWaitForTime … CurrentDockForDuration 601.0, TimeSinceLastLoadTransferUpdate 564.6`. (2026-10-14, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- **A save taken while a train is docked does not keep the docking on a dedicated server: after the load the train is undocked and its timetable sends it on to the next stop, so a load phase in progress is skipped (the train leaves empty).** Saved 0.5 s after the docking at `<station A>` (docking state docked, 0 km/h), loaded on the server: the train drove empty to `<station B>` and docked there. `mDockingState` is not saved ("updated from the station we're docked to on load", `FGTrain.h`): right after the load, where a mod's agents start, the train does not read as undocked yet, and it drops to `TDS_None` later without a docking-state broadcast, so a check at start-up misses it. What worked: a per-frame check of every train the mod has in a load or unload phase, and when its docking state is `TDS_None` and the timetable's current stop is not the docking stop, `SetCurrentStop(<that stop>)` and the autopilot off and on: the train docked again where it stood, loaded 3200 and delivered. Evidence: server log `docked at <station B> in phase <loading> …` and the test line `<train> leaves the supplier with cargo 0`. (2026-10-23, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- **[?] A docking saved in its load phase comes back at `ETPDS_WaitingForTransfer`: the transfer happens about 0.4 s into the loaded world.** A save made 5 s into a 23.86 s loading phase (the platform in `ETPDS_Loading`), loaded three times in one process from the main menu: each time the first frame watched showed `WaitingForTransfer -> Complete`, the wagon got its 3200 at once (`wagon +3200, platform -3198`, 2 more items arriving from the belt in that frame) and the train left 7 s after the load, so none of the remaining 19 s of the phase was played. Seen with a mod that shortens the phase timers, which fall back to the defaults after a load; a run without any mod was not made, hence the marker. Evidence: `fast: t=0.4 <station A>.1 WaitingForTransfer -> Complete after 1.4 s`, `load 1: the docking ended 7 s after the load, wagon 3200 of 3200`. (2026-10-28, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
- **On a client, a station's platforms cannot be found through the station's platform connections: the walk from `AFGBuildableRailroadStation::GetStationOutputConnection()` over the connected platforms returns nothing (the connections' links do not replicate).** `AFGRailroadSubsystem::GetAllTrainStations` and the station names do replicate. A client that needs the cargo platforms iterates `AFGBuildableTrainPlatformCargo` actors (and gets the server's choice by replication where it depends on the station). Evidence: a client watch found `0 cargo platforms` by the station walk and 8 by actor iteration on the same save. (2026-10-23, game 513885, SML 3.12.0)
