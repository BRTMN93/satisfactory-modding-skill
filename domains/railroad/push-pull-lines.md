# Railways: push-pull trains and two-way single-track lines

Part of the `satisfactory-modding` skill, railroad domain (index: `domains/railroad/README.md`); routes of your own for the autopilot are in `domains/railroad/own-routes.md`. Markers: no marker = verified in practice, **[source]** = read from SML or game code, not tested, **[?]** = uncertain. Replace the parts in `<...>` with your own.

## Push-pull trains and two-way single-track lines

- **Push-pull trains (a locomotive at each end) on a two-way single-track line: timetable, docking, own routes and parking work as on a loop.**
  - Two such trains ran five round trips each between two end stations that face opposite ways, passing each other on a one-way passing loop: 20 dockings, no autopilot error, 0.4 s of standstill per leg, 576 m in 37–43 s.
  - Docking: `TDS_ReadyToDock` and `TDS_Docked` in one tick. The leading locomotive stands at the station building, the wagon on the first platform, the rear locomotive on the second (an empty platform). When the docking ends, the game finds the next route from the locomotive at the other end: its first point is the far end of the track under that locomotive. Nothing has to be done to reverse the train.
  - A train also leaves backwards from plain track: parked with the autopilot off in front of a signal, then given a timetable stop behind it and the autopilot, it moved 0.6 s later on a route from its rear locomotive (five times out of five).
  - `mAtcData.SetPath` works on two-way track: a route cut at the exit path signal of a loop track stopped the train 8.0 m before it, five times, with the other train passing on the other loop track without stopping.
  - `AFGTrain::mSimulationData.FirstVehicle` is not the vehicle that leads in the driving direction: it stayed on the same vehicle while the train drove with the other end first.
  - **The docking time of a stop that moves no cargo is its `DockingRuleSet.DockForDuration`.** With the none descriptor as load and unload filter and the default docking definition, a stop took 15 s (the default duration) and 1.0–1.1 s with `DockForDuration = 1.f`. Test timetables that only need the stop to happen can use that to save 14 s per stop. The same-frame docking end with the none filter as the only load filter (the "nothing" filter, above) was a different setting; why the two differ was not investigated.
  - A timetable stop the pathfinder cannot reach gives the error `SDLE_StationUnreachable` at once; the train stands with `SDLS_Idle` and no route, and the error clears when the autopilot is switched off.
  - Evidence (game 502094): `finished: 1 steps, autopilot errors 0`, `docked at … vehicles: 0 locomotive on T21 …, 1 wagon on T22 …, 2 locomotive on T23`, `stopped 8.0 m before parking signal S19 (RSA_Stop)`, `event … error SDLE_StationUnreachable`. (2026-10-03, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **The locomotives a route can start from have a free front coupler.** `!Locomotive->IsCoupledAt(ERailroadVehicleCoupler::RVC_FRONT)` picked, on 8 trains (locomotive + wagons, two locomotives + wagons, locomotive-wagon-locomotive), a set of at most two locomotives that reached every station any locomotive of the train reached with `FindPathSync`: the head locomotive, and the rear one of a push-pull train (`IsOrientationReversed()` 1, track position `Forward` -1). The second of two locomotives at the head has its front coupled and is left out. So a mod need not ask from every locomotive of a long train. Evidence: `vehicle 2 locomotive … forward -1, reversed 1, coupled front 0 back 1, can lead`, `vehicle 1 locomotive … coupled front 1 back 1` (not leading), 0 trains whose leading locomotives reached fewer stations than all its locomotives. (2026-10-05, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
- **The game's signalling alone does not protect a two-way single-track stretch that is split into several blocks.** On a line where the stretch between a passing loop and an end station was split by the signals of a switch, and the station was a block of its own:
  - a train that followed another one to the end station drove up to the station's entry signal and stopped there; the first train, done with docking, could not leave because the block in front of the station was taken. Both stood at red signals with `TimeWaitingAtSignal` counting and no autopilot error (watched for 60 s; the long-wait error comes at 300 s).
  - a train that followed a train parked on a one-way loop track stopped 10 m before the path signal in front of the loop's switch, on the two-way track, and so closed the line for both directions.
  - **[source]** The rule players give for this: every two-way stretch between two passing places is one undivided block, the end station included, or its entry is a path signal chained up to the station (**[?]** this sits badly with "no path signal directly before a station" above; the signal kind at a station entry was not tested) (Steam discussions; signal guide on docs.ficsit.app). Not tested here.
  - Evidence (game 502094): `head-on standstill, both trains have stood for 60 s with the autopilot on: … waiting at signal 56 s, next signal T21.0 RSA_Stop 8 m … || … waiting at signal 62 s, next signal T03.1 RSA_Stop 10 m`. (2026-10-03, game 502094, SML 3.12.0)
